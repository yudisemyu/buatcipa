# Content Guidelines

Every paragraph should sound natural.

Avoid poetic language that feels forced.

Write as if speaking directly to one person.

Keep memories short.

Focus on genuine moments.

Do not exaggerate.

---

# Tone of Voice

Gentle

Playful

Honest

Hopeful

Never dramatic.

Never cringe.
